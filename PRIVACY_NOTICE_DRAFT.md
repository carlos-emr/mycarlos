## Your records on your device

myCarlos is a patient-run local application. You control your device and vault and choose which records to add, which supported connections to use, and where to save copies. This control does not extend to records held by a connected portal, clinic, support provider or another service you choose.

myCarlos lets you keep documents and photos in an encrypted vault on your device. The vault can contain document names and contents, folders, information about the people whose records you manage, saved AI answers, clinic links and delivery records. You choose which files to add and which people to keep records for. Make sure you are entitled to manage someone else's information.

The app unlocks the vault using your passphrase or recovery key. Keep those secrets private and keep your recovery kit somewhere safe. The publisher does not provide an account password-reset service for your vault. Losing both ways to unlock it can mean losing access to your records.

Encryption protects stored contents, but it does not conceal everything. Someone with access to the vault files or a backup may still see that a vault exists, file sizes and dates, and some recovery and encryption settings. Information is readable while you use the unlocked app. Device security, screen access and the applications you share files with also matter.

The device keeps some app preferences and recovery-workflow reminders outside the encrypted vault. These can include language, appearance, text size, and details of a backup you selected, such as its file name. On Windows builds where document notices are supported, the device also keeps notice settings and a limited record of notice outcomes outside the encrypted vault. That record uses hashed references to arrivals rather than document titles or contents. **[PUBLISHER TO CONFIRM THE FINAL RELEASE'S DEVICE STORAGE AND OPERATING SYSTEM BACKUP BEHAVIOR.]**

## Connections to a clinic portal

If this build supports it and you choose to link a clinic portal, myCarlos connects to that portal to link your device, check for documents, download copies and send delivery receipts. The portal can associate your device with your portal account. It receives device public keys and identifiers, connection times and your internet address, along with delivery and receipt information. It may retain records after you remove a link.

Clinic delivery packages are encrypted for the receiving device. myCarlos checks received packages before adding accepted documents to the vault. This does not make all portal records private from the portal operator: accounts and delivery metadata are handled separately. The clinic and portal notice explains those uses. You can remove a supported clinic link. This does not erase documents already saved in your vault or records held by the clinic.

Enabled document checks contact the portal. Your operating system may display a document notice. **[CONFIRM WHICH CONNECTIONS, BACKGROUND CHECKS AND NOTIFICATION SERVICES THIS RELEASE ENABLES, THEIR PROVIDERS AND LOCATIONS.]** Linking and delivery availability vary by build and platform.

## AI features and model downloads

Where supported, Summary uses an AI model on your device. It does not upload the document to a hosted AI service. Saved answers are kept encrypted with the document. An answer can be wrong; check it against the original and ask your care team about medical decisions.

If you choose to download a model, the app contacts its download host and permitted delivery hosts. They receive your internet address and request information, including which model file is requested and when. The model files currently come from Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure. **[CONFIRM HOSTS, THEIR PRIVACY NOTICE LINKS AND ANY RELEASE CHANGES.]** This network download is separate from processing your document locally.

You can also save text with identifying details removed for use elsewhere. Removal can miss names, dates and other identifying details, including text you did not notice in the original. Review the whole preview and remove more where needed. Saving this file does not send it to an AI service. If you later upload it, the service you choose handles it under its own terms and privacy notice.

## Copies outside the vault

An imported file may remain in its original location or in the app that shared it. A readable export, exported text or recovery kit is outside the vault's protection. Other people, applications, synced folders or cloud services may receive copies depending on where you save or share it. myCarlos cannot retrieve or erase those copies.

You can save an encrypted backup. It includes retained vault information, including hidden documents and history. Protect it and the secrets that open it. Operating system backups may also keep app data, depending on the platform. Changing a passphrase or recovery key does not change older backups or vaults already set aside.

## What stays when you hide or replace something

Hiding a document does not delete it. It stays encrypted in the vault and in later backups. Older saved AI answers can also remain hidden. The vault keeps an encrypted history of actions, which can include names, earlier names and saved file locations. The app does not offer deletion of that history.

Starting again or replacing the current vault with a backup sets the old vault aside rather than erasing it. Anyone who can reach an old vault or backup and has its old unlocking secret may still open it. Removal outside the app depends on your platform and may remove all app data. Ask for the instructions for your device before deleting anything.

There is no promised automatic expiry for your vault records. **[PUBLISHER TO CONFIRM PLATFORM REMOVAL INSTRUCTIONS AND ANY RELEASE RETENTION CHANGES.]** The clinic's record-retention rules are separate.
